About StayScout

Revolutionizing hostel booking in Kotli with AI-powered intelligence

Our Mission

StayScout KOTLI was born from a simple idea: finding the right hostel shouldn't be hard. We combine cutting-edge machine learning with deep local knowledge to connect guests with their perfect stay while helping hostel owners maximize their potential.

Our Story

Developed by SHOAIB, StayScout started as an academic project to solve real hostel booking problems in Kotli, AJK. What began as a simple idea has grown into a full platform featuring:

  • AI Recommendations — 85% match accuracy
  • Demand Prediction — MAE of 8.2% for the next 30 days
  • Dynamic Pricing — +22% revenue potential for owners
  • Cancellation Risk Scoring — AUC of 0.87
  • Sentiment Analysis — Real-time review insights
  • Anomaly Detection — Protecting users from fraud

Our Values

Built with modern web technologies: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6+), Chart.js for visualizations, and simulated ML models including Demand Prediction, Sentiment Analysis, and Recommendation Engine.
